---
title: How objectives are kept
type: page
---

An objective is a task with key results under it — `parent:`, because that is
hierarchy and a board reads it. Ordinary work points at a key result with
`contributes_to:`, because that is an annotation and not a chain of command: a
task usually serves one, sometimes two, and often none at all.

## Progress is not a percentage of tasks

A key result carries `current` and `target`, both measured outside this vault.
Counting finished tasks would make a busy quarter look like a successful one,
which is the specific failure OKRs were invented to prevent.

## What has no objective

Most of the work, most of the time, and that is not a fault. Keeping the lights
on contributes to nothing on this list and still has to happen.

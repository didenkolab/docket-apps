A risk is a task of type `risk`. It says how likely it is and how bad it would
be, who is watching it, and what it threatens — as a link, so the thing at risk
shows it in its backlinks.

## No score

Likelihood times impact gives a number, and a number gives a ranking, and a
ranking gets sorted and cut off at a line. The two words are kept as words on
purpose: "likely / severe" is a sentence somebody can argue with, and "12" is
not.

## Closing one

A risk is done when it can no longer happen — the provider was replaced, the
release shipped, the season passed. A risk that merely has not happened yet is
still open, and moving it to Done because nothing went wrong is how a register
becomes fiction.

## What was asked

In their words, as close as you can. Translating it into what you think they
meant is the first place a request goes wrong.

## What they are trying to do

Why they want it. This is the part that lets somebody propose the cheaper thing
that solves the same problem.

## What we did about it

Link the work with `became:`, or say why not. A request that is quietly dropped
teaches the person not to ask again, which is the expensive outcome.

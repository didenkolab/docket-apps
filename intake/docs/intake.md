---
title: How requests are kept
type: page
---

A request is not a task. It is somebody outside the team saying what they want,
and it stays in their words until the team decides what, if anything, to do.

The work it becomes is a separate task, linked with `became:`. So the request
can be answered — "this became ACME-412, shipped in March" — and refused
requests keep their reason instead of vanishing.

## Why not just make a task

Because a task is a commitment and a request is not. Turning every request into
a backlog item makes a backlog nobody can read and a queue nobody is really in;
refusing to record it at all makes people ask twice, louder, elsewhere.

An hour spent is a note, not a number on a card.

Write a task of type `worklog`, say how many hours and on what date, and point
it at the work with `logs:`. The task being worked on then shows every entry in
its backlinks, and a person's page shows everything they logged.

## Why not a field on the task

Because `spent: 14` answers none of the questions time tracking exists for. Who
spent them, on which days, and on what exactly — an invoice needs all three, and
a correction needs to change one entry without touching the rest. A number on a
card can only be overwritten, and an overwritten number has no history anybody
can read.

## What this app does not decide

Whether hours are estimated against, billed, or reported anywhere. Those are
decisions, and a pack that made them would be a pack that had opinions about
your contracts.
